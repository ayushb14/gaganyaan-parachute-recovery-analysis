# Assumptions and Limitations

- Public and assumed values were used where exact ADRDE/ISRO parameters were unavailable.
- Canopy geometries are simplified fixed 3D representations.
- Fabric flexibility, porosity, line dynamics, and true FSI are not modelled.
- OpenFOAM cases are stage-wise steady RANS approximations.
- MATLAB Monte Carlo results represent uncertainty propagation for early-stage research analysis.
